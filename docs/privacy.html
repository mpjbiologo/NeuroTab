Português

Resumo: a NeuroTab não tem servidor, não tem conta, não usa análise de uso (analytics) e não vende nem compartilha dados. Tudo o que você cria (atalhos, notas, tarefas, fotos, configurações) fica guardado no seu navegador, no armazenamento local da extensão.

O que sai do seu computador, e só quando você usa o recurso

Esses serviços têm políticas de privacidade próprias. A NeuroTab não envia identificadores, e-mail nem histórico de navegação a nenhum deles.

Permissões

Cotações, endereços do Trânsito, fotos do Bing/NASA, histórico de mais visitados, abas fechadas e acesso a outros sites são opcionais: o navegador pede sua autorização na primeira vez que você usa o recurso. As chaves de API ficam só no seu navegador e só entram no backup exportado se você marcar essa opção (com senha opcional para criptografá-las).

Seus dados, seu controle

Você pode exportar tudo (Configurações → Backup), apagar notas e tarefas, esvaziar a lixeira ou remover a extensão, o que apaga os dados locais. O código é aberto: github.com/mpjbiologo/NeuroTab.

Contato

Dúvidas: abra uma issue em github.com/mpjbiologo/NeuroTab/issues.

English

Summary: NeuroTab has no server, no account and no analytics, and it does not sell or share data. Everything you create (shortcuts, notes, tasks, photos, settings) is stored in your browser, in the extension's local storage.

What leaves your computer, and only when you use the feature

FeatureWhere it goesWhat is sent
Weather and time zonesOpen-MeteoCoordinates of the cities you chose; the name you type when searching for a city
QuotesAwesomeAPI, brapi.devThe currency and stock codes you chose
TrafficOpenStreetMap Nominatim; embedded Waze mapThe address you type when searching; coordinates of the place shown on the map
RoutesGoogle Maps or Waze (new tab)Origin and destination, only when you click the route
Photo slideshowBing, NASANo personal data, only the request for the photo list. Photos from your computer stay in the browser
Image libraryThe site the image comes fromOnly the download of the image you asked to save (with your permission for that site). The image stays in the browser
NewsThe RSS feeds you addOnly the feed request
Artificial intelligenceThe provider you set up (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, local Ollama or other)The text of the note or question and excerpts of your notes used as context, with your API key. With no key set, nothing is sent
Search by meaning (optional)The same AI provider you set upNote text, to build the index. The index stays in the browser
Dictation and voice commandsThe browser's own speech recognitionAudio is processed by the browser (in Chrome and Edge it may go through the vendor's servers)
Wake word (optional, off by default)The browser's speech recognitionContinuous audio while the new tab is open and focused, until it turns itself off for lack of use
Update noticeGitHubOnly the latest-version lookup
Site icons (Firefox only)DuckDuckGoThe domain of your shortcuts, to show the icon

These services have their own privacy policies. NeuroTab sends no identifiers, e-mail or browsing history to any of them.

Permissions

Quotes, Traffic addresses, Bing/NASA photos, top sites, recently closed tabs and access to other sites are optional: the browser asks you the first time you use the feature. API keys stay in your browser and only go into an exported backup if you tick that option (with an optional password to encrypt them).

Your data, your control

You can export everything (Settings → Backup), delete notes and tasks, empty the trash or remove the extension, which deletes the local data. The code is open source: github.com/mpjbiologo/NeuroTab.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/mpjbiologo/NeuroTab/issues.
